# Limitations

BocaBind `v0.1.0` deliberately does not model:

- ligand rotation, torsions, or protonation;
- side-chain, loop, or backbone motion;
- curved or branched routes;
- water, ions, electrostatics, entropy, or free energy;
- biological assembly selection;
- actual association or dissociation kinetics;
- induced fit versus conformational selection.

PDB coordinate uncertainty, missing residues, modeled atoms, alternate conformations, unusual elements, covalent ligands, and incorrect biological assemblies can change the result. Always inspect the structure and trajectory before interpreting a classification.
